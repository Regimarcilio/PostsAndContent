O consumo da tecnologia acontece fora da tela — mas as decisões de produto começam dentro dela.

Em 2025, data centers consumiram quase 500 TWh de eletricidade. A IEA estima crescimento de 17% no ano e projeta cerca de 950 TWh em 2030. É uma projeção de consumo elétrico, não uma medida direta de emissões. Fonte: IEA, https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary

Fontes ITU: faixa de estimativas https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/backgrounders/Pages/climate-change.aspx; dados empresariais https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/Pages/PR-2026-09-02-greening-digital-companies.aspx

Na prática, equipes podem:
• medir o que cada serviço consome;
• reduzir computação repetida e armazenamento sem propósito;
• avaliar fornecedores e prolongar a vida útil dos equipamentos.

O primeiro passo não é buscar um número único. É declarar o que está sendo medido — e usar isso para decidir melhor. #TecnologiaResponsável

🇺🇸 English

Technology's energy use happens beyond the screen; product decisions start on it.

Data centers used nearly 500 TWh of electricity in 2025. The IEA estimates 17% growth that year and projects about 950 TWh by 2030. That is an electricity forecast, not a direct measure of emissions. Source: IEA, https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary

Estimates for ICT's share of global emissions range from 0.8% to 4%, depending on boundaries and methods. In 2024, 200 digital companies assessed by the ITU reported 301 Mt CO₂e of operational Scope 1 and 2 emissions; only 47% reported all relevant Scope 3 activities. That sample does not represent the whole sector by itself. ITU sources: estimate range https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/backgrounders/Pages/climate-change.aspx; company data https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/Pages/PR-2026-09-02-greening-digital-companies.aspx

For teams building digital services:
• measure what each service uses;
• reduce repeated computation and unnecessary storage;
• assess providers and keep equipment useful longer.

The first step is not chasing one universal number. It is stating what is measured, then using that information to make better decisions. #ResponsibleTech
